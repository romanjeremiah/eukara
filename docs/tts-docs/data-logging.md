Google works continuously to improve its products, including the use of customer data from Google consumer data to refine those products in many cases. Cloud Text-to-Speech is both stateless and resourceless. This means [`Data Access`](/logging/docs/audit#data-access) and [`System Event`](/logging/docs/audit#system-event) data don't apply. As a result, Cloud TTS is out of the scope of Client Access Licenses (CAL). Google does not log any customer Cloud TTS text or audio data.
